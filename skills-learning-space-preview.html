How your Skills Refresher course works

Each part of the course prepares you for the next. Your instructor will add approved learning materials and skills instructions as they are assigned.

1

Prepare

Check the weekend schedule, review assigned materials, and write down questions before class.

2

Learn

Participate in the scheduled theory instruction and connect the material to safe nursing practice.

3

Practice

Use instructor guidance and assigned practice to prepare for the hands-on portion of the course.

4

Check off

Complete required in-person skills check-off activities with the instructor. Arrive on time and follow the posted instructions.

Six-week journey

Your course weekends

The course is scheduled across six weeks. Exact daily start/end times remain pending ITASA confirmation.

Weekend 1Orientation, assigned learning, and instructor guidance.
Weekend 2Continue assigned learning and prepare for supervised skills work.
Weekend 3Build confidence through review, questions, and instructor-guided practice.
Weekend 4Continue course learning and skills preparation using assigned materials.
Weekend 5Focus on readiness for required skills check-off activities.
Weekend 6Complete remaining course requirements and review completion status.

Course purpose & professional responsibility

The Skills Refresher course is provided for educational purposes. Completion does not replace state licensing requirements, clinical experience, employer orientation, work-specific competency validation, or any requirements imposed by a licensing authority.

Students remain responsible for ensuring that their education and training meet applicable laws, Board of Nursing requirements, employer policies, and professional scope-of-practice standards.
