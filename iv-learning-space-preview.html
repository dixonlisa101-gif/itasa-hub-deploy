How each lesson will teach you

Lesson design preview

The six-module teaching draft is in staff review. Lessons and activities will appear here after ITASA approves their release.

  1. Learn

    Begin with a clear learning goal and a short explanation of the topic, with examples and the reasons behind each decision.

  2. Walk through

    Read a short practice situation and notice the decisions that matter.

  3. Practice

    Work through a guided scenario or an instructor-supervised activity using the lesson's approved materials.

  4. Explain

    Describe your reasoning, receive feedback, and revisit anything that needs more practice.

  5. Demonstrate

    Use the instructor's checklist during supervised skills practice and competency validation.

Review questions will support the lessons. Opening materials or reading answers does not record attendance, demonstrate a skill, or complete the course.
