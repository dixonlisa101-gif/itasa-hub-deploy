Your place to learn, prepare, and keep moving forward

ITASA classes are delivered through Zoom-based instruction. Some classes also include a required in-person skills check-off or hands-on competency phase.

  • Join your scheduled Zoom-based instruction
  • Complete required in-person skills check-offs when your class includes them
  • Arrive on time for all required in-person skills sessions
  • Return to the Learning Center for class access, materials, updates, scheduled sessions, and support

Location privacy: Physical skills-location details are not displayed publicly. They are provided to registered students after payment confirmation when their class requires an in-person component.

ITASA Learning Center Disclaimer

ITASA Learning Center courses, including the NCLEX Review Course, Skills Refresher Course, and IV Insertion & Venipuncture Certification Course, are provided for educational and training purposes only.

Completion of an ITASA class does not guarantee NCLEX success, nursing licensure, employment, satisfaction of any remediation or re-entry requirements imposed by a licensing authority, or authorization to practice nursing or perform clinical procedures.

Skills training does not replace state licensing requirements, clinical experience, employer orientation, or work-specific competency validation.

Students are responsible for ensuring that the education and training they receive meet applicable state laws, Board of Nursing requirements, employer policies, and professional scope-of-practice standards.

ITASA Learning Center is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NCSBN, Pearson VUE, or any state Board of Nursing.
